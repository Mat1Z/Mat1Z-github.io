Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Pult Stats accesses only the account owner's basic profile information and statistics of their own videos (views, likes, comments, shares), using the scopes user.info.basic, user.info.stats and video.list.

This data is stored only on the owner's own computer, is not shared with or sold to third parties, and is not used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions. Stored data can be deleted by removing the local database.
